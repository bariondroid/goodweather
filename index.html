Privacy policy

Updated 24 November 2026

This policy covers the official Android app and these project pages. The developer and privacy contact is BarionDroid, publishing as BarionDroid. Email: bariondroid@gmail.com.

Good Weather, package com.bariondroid.good.weather, does not require an app account. The current release is free, has no advertising or in-app purchases, and does not request an advertising identifier. The developer does not sell personal data or run a separate app analytics service. Test-only builds can include development integrations.

This page describes every feature. A build may leave some out, such as radar, weather history, Ask AI with CSV, widgets or notifications; the related data flows then do not occur.

Data on your device

The app stores the last selected place, favourite places, the last successful forecast, language preference, and widget settings in internal app storage. A history archive is cached for up to 12 selected coordinates and refreshed at most once per 24 hours. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes it. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for this data.

Ask AI with CSV first displays a confirmation describing the location, coordinates and archive size. After you confirm, Android's system share sheet lets you choose an installed recipient that accepts CSV sharing. The app does not assign a trusted AI brand to package names or app labels, authenticate the chosen recipient's publisher, or upload a list of installed apps. A separate CSV file in the private cache contains the full available five-year archive, selected location name and coordinates, daily weather estimates, data-source version and access time. Your question and selected-period context accompany it; selecting a shorter period does not shorten the attachment. Only your chosen recipient receives temporary read access to that file. Check the attachment and question in that app before sending; receivers may handle attachments differently. Cancel either step to stop sharing. Later exports do not overwrite earlier files. On a new export, the app keeps at most 12 exports and removes files older than seven days. Android may clear cache sooner.

A custom widget image remains in its original location. The app stores only its Android content URI and reads a bounded copy when it renders the widget. Removing the widget removes its settings and releases its retained image permission when no other widget uses the URI.

Location

Approximate and precise location permissions are optional. After you select Use my location, the app reads coordinates and uses them for forecasts and nearby observations through the providers listed below. Android's system Geocoder can also receive the coordinates to name the place. The app does not track your movement or read a new device location in the background. Widgets and enabled alerts can reuse the saved coordinates with the app closed. These transfers are explained beside the location action and in notification settings.

Network communication

  • Open-Meteo receives place search terms and selected coordinates, including for archive data such as recent snowfall, past days and last year. The default place is London. Opening the app may fetch the default or last selected place even without device-location permission. Widgets, morning briefings and enabled weather notifications can refresh forecasts in an Android background job using the last selected coordinates; they do not request a new device location. Android may delay refreshes while offline or conserving battery. Open-Meteo states that free API logs may contain coordinates and are deleted after 90 days.
  • Deutscher Wetterdienst receives forecast-map metadata, legend and map-tile requests. These disclose the visible map area, forecast time and normal HTTPS technical data such as your IP address. The map no longer sends a 49-point coordinate grid to Open-Meteo. See DWD data-use information.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the other project pages. The app does not download forecast data from GitHub Pages; it only opens these pages in your browser when you choose a legal link, and the browser request exposes normal HTTPS technical data such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • After you select Weather history or Load archive, NASA POWER receives the selected coordinates and requested five-year date range when the cached archive needs a refresh. It returns daily model and satellite grid estimates. The app stores the response only in its bounded on-device cache. NASA POWER data are not local-station measurements. See the NASA POWER Daily API and referencing guide.
  • In Czechia, ČHMÚ provides public automatic-station observations. The app selects nearby station IDs on the device and does not send the selected coordinates to ČHMÚ. ČHMÚ servers process normal HTTPS technical data such as your IP address and requested public file names.
  • AviationWeather receives a bounded coordinate box around the selected location to find recent worldwide METAR observations, including in Czechia. The request reveals the approximate selected area and normal HTTPS technical data such as your IP address. See the AviationWeather Data API.
  • OpenStreetMap receives requests for visible tiles while the point picker or observed radar map is open. RainViewer receives requests for the radar manifest, visible radar tiles, and coverage-mask tiles. Tile requests reveal the approximate visible map area and normal HTTPS technical data such as your IP address. See the RainViewer Weather Maps API.
  • Release builds do not contact Google Mobile Ads, UMP, or Play Billing and do not expose Premium purchases.
  • Buy Me a Coffee receives data only if you choose the optional external support action.

All app network communication uses HTTPS. The app does not send contacts, messages, photos, audio, or the contents of a selected widget image. A weather-history CSV leaves the app only after you confirm sharing and choose an AI app or a recipient in Android's share sheet. That recipient processes the file under its own terms.

Retention, deletion and choices

There is no Good Weather account to delete. To remove all local saved places, settings, cached forecasts and archive files, open Android Settings > Apps > Good Weather > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Android may display the launcher name Weather. Menu labels vary by device. Files already copied to another app, provider logs and support correspondence are separate; use the provider's controls or contact support for information held by the developer.

The developer does not operate a server that stores app data. Data on your device remains until the cache replaces it, you clear app data, or you uninstall the app. You can remove location permission in Android settings. You can remove image access by deleting the widget, changing its image, or managing the selected document in Android. Open-Meteo, GitHub, NASA POWER, ČHMÚ, DWD, AviationWeather, OpenStreetMap, RainViewer, Buy Me a Coffee, and a share-sheet recipient process data under their own terms when you contact their services.

Notification preferences and bounded delivery fingerprints stay on your device. From version 0.4.3, background alerts and morning briefings require a separate in-app choice before saved coordinates can be used for those background requests, including on existing installations. Dismissing or declining the disclosure leaves them off. You can change the choice in Notifications. Previous category preferences and briefing time are kept; rain advice may be initially selected but cannot run without this choice. Android notification permission separately controls notification delivery. Widgets you explicitly add refresh separately, as explained before adding or applying a widget. Each alert category can be disabled in the app or independently in Android settings. Android may delay enabled checks; they do not track your movement.

Some providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. Their policies describe their own practices and safeguards. The developer cannot remotely delete a provider's logs or an AI recipient's copy. Declining future sharing does not retrieve previously shared data.

Purposes, support and privacy rights

Data is used to supply the weather, widget, history and export functions you request. Optional device access and sharing remain under your control. If you email support, your address, message and attachments are processed to answer and resolve your request; they are not used to enrol you in marketing. If the weather servers cannot be reached, Tell the developer contacts the weather and map hosts listed in the app's report to test whether they respond, which exposes your IP address to them, and then opens a prefilled email in your email app. The email lists your network and SIM country codes, language setting, time zone, app and Android versions, phone manufacturer and model, network type, VPN state, the error and the result for each host. It contains no coordinates. Nothing is emailed unless you review and send it. Keep unrelated personal information out of support messages and public GitHub issues.

Where GDPR applies, the bases for the developer's processing are providing the service you request (Article 6(1)(b)), your consent for the optional disclosure through the sharing flow (Article 6(1)(a)), and legitimate interests in responding to support and protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f)). Handling statutory requests or record-keeping duties may rely on a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request or an associated dispute, or to meet a legal duty. These bases do not replace a provider's own privacy obligations.

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Consent may be withdrawn for future processing without invalidating earlier lawful processing. Email bariondroid@gmail.com to exercise these rights. Requests are generally due a response within one month, subject to lawful exceptions described by the Czech Data Protection Authority.

You can complain to your local supervisory authority, including the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů in Czechia. The developer has no remote access to your device-only archive. Weather calculations do not make legal or similarly significant decisions about you.
